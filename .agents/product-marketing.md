## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Synarava is a curated online shop for jewelry and characterful objects selected for craft, material, symbolism, and everyday wear.

**What it does:** Synarava sells a Shopify-backed catalog through a custom editorial storefront. **Product category:** Curated jewelry shop / artisan accessories / editorial e-commerce.

**Product type:** E-commerce storefront with a lightweight CMS and Shopify commerce backend.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Not B2B. This is a consumer storefront.

**Decision-makers:** Individual buyers choosing jewelry, accessories, and curated objects for themselves or as gifts.

**Primary use case:** Help customers discover meaningful, well-made pieces by object type, material, collection, symbolism, occasion, and availability.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Find a jewelry piece or accessory with enough character to feel personal.
- Understand materials, fit, care, and symbolism before buying.
- Choose a gift or keepsake that feels considered rather than generic.

**Use cases:**
- Buying jewelry for everyday wear.
- Choosing a symbolic piece tied to a material, form, or collection story.
- Browsing new arrivals and curated edits.
- Comparing pieces by category, product type, availability, material, finish, origin, and compliance signals.
- Returning to saved products or account history after signing in through Shopify Customer Accounts.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Jewelry and accessory shopping often collapses into generic product grids, thin descriptions, and unclear material or care information.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Marketplaces can bury craft, symbolism, and material context under filters and price sorting.
- Conventional jewelry pages often separate commerce details from the emotional reason to choose the piece.
- Social discovery can create interest but leaves shoppers without structured product, care, and checkout information.

**What it costs them:** Time, doubt, abandoned carts, and purchases that feel less personal than intended.

**Emotional tension:** The customer wants the piece to feel instinctive, considered, and lasting, but still needs practical confidence before buying.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Independent jewelry stores and curated accessory shops — often fall short when product pages lack rich editorial context or multilingual SEO foundations.

**Secondary:** Large marketplaces and fashion retailers — often offer breadth but less distinctive storytelling, provenance, or symbolic framing.

**Indirect:** Social platforms, gift guides, personal recommendations, and offline boutiques — can inspire discovery but usually do not provide a complete purchase and post-purchase path.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Shopify-native commerce with a custom editorial storefront instead of a generic theme-first shop.
- Separate Synarava editorial layer for symbolism, materials, process, lookbook, and collection storytelling.
- Structured product discovery by category, type, collection, material, finish, origin, availability, and compliance.
- Multilingual content infrastructure with English, Portuguese, and Russian support planned/partially in place.
- Operational SEO tooling in the admin: SERP previews, meta health checks, redirects visibility, sitemap, robots, JSON-LD, and image alt checks.

**How we do it differently:** Commerce facts stay canonical in Shopify, while Synarava adds a curated narrative layer around the object. Copy should connect what the item is, what it is made from, what it symbolizes, how it wears, and why it belongs in the collection.

**Why that's better:** Customers get both practical buying confidence and a stronger reason to care about a piece.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Is this material safe / durable / comfortable? | Use specific material, fit, care, compliance, and construction details from Shopify/Synarava fields. Do not make unsupported claims. |
| Will this fit my style or the recipient? | Ground copy in form, occasion, scale, styling, and collection context. |
| Can I trust checkout, shipping, and returns? | Shopify powers customer accounts, cart, checkout, order flow, and purchase facts; legal, shipping, returns, care, and FAQ pages exist. |
| Why this piece instead of a cheaper marketplace alternative? | Emphasize curation, material notes, symbolic intent, and the specific product story. Avoid vague luxury language. |

**Anti-persona:** Shoppers looking only for the lowest possible price, anonymous bulk accessories, or products without material/story context.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Generic product grids, thin descriptions, uncertainty about materials/care, and uninspiring gift options.

**Pull:** A curated selection, product stories, material notes, collection framing, and a checkout path backed by Shopify.

**Habit:** Buyers may default to marketplaces, familiar jewelry retailers, Instagram discovery, or offline boutiques.

**Anxiety:** Concerns about material quality, fit, shipping, returns, authenticity of claims, and whether the piece will feel as distinctive in person.

## SEO & Content Direction

**Priority demand types:**
- Product intent: material + object + use/recipient.
- Meaning intent: symbols, provenance, craft, and cultural context.
- Care intent: sizing, materials, storage, cleaning, allergies, and durability.
- Occasion intent: gifting, styling, seasonal moments, and collections.

**Copy rule:** Start with the specific product fact, then add the editorial reason to care. Do not invent provenance, certification, cultural meaning, customer quotes, or metrics.

**Publishing rule:** Search-driven briefs, internal-link suggestions, stale-page detection, and content refreshes can be generated automatically, but final cultural claims, product promises, and brand wording require editorial approval.
